## 3. Recommended Final Set of 8 Companies

To fulfill the SPEC criteria of **5 Clean + 2–3 Messy companies** that exercise every rule in RULES.md and SPEC.md §9 (accounting equation derivation, NCI handling, missing Gross Profit handling, and segment checks):

1. **AAPL (Clean):** Gold-standard hardware/services filer; cleanly tabulates all 8 metrics with standard labels.
2. **MSFT (Clean):** Enterprise cloud filer; clean 3-year comparative layout, all 8 metrics explicitly reported.
3. **NKE (Clean):** Consumer/retail filer with off-calendar fiscal year end (May 31); standard 8 metrics.
4. **JNJ (Clean):** Healthcare/pharma filer; clean balance sheet and income statement under `Sales to customers`.
5. **GOOGL (Clean / Controlled Messy):** Exercises the `Gross profit -> not_reported` rule while keeping clean Total Liabilities and standard equity.
6. **KO (Messy #1):** Exercises the `Total liabilities -> derived` accounting equation derivation ($Assets - Equity$) and NCI equity attribution.
7. **AMZN (Messy #2):** Exercises **both** `Total liabilities -> derived` and `Gross profit -> not_reported` simultaneously.
8. **PEP (Messy #3):** Exercises NCI reconciliation (Parent Net Income vs Consolidated Net Income) and multi-part Cash Flow HTML structures.
